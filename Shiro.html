Description

An easy, classic homemade lasagna uses a rich meat sauce, a creamy cheese layer, tender pasta, and plenty of melted mozzarella.

Ingredients

  • 1 lb ground beef and 8 oz Italian sausage
  • 1 jar (32 oz) marinara or pasta sauce
  • 1 onion, finely diced
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • Salt and black pepper to taste
  • 15 oz ricotta cheese (or cottage cheese)
  • 1 large egg
  • 1 cup grated Parmesan cheese
  • 1 tsp dried Italian seasoning
  • 12–15 lasagna noodles (boiled or oven-ready)
  • 4 cups shredded mozzarella cheese (divided)

Steps

```html
  1. Preheat and boil: Heat your oven to 375°F (190°C). If using standard noodles, boil them in a large pot of salted water until al dente, then drain.
  2. Make the meat sauce: Heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat. Cook the ground beef, sausage, and onion until browned (6 to 8 minutes). Add the garlic and cook for 1 minute, then stir in the marinara sauce and simmer for 5 minutes.
  3. Mix the cheese: In a medium bowl, stir together the ricotta cheese, beaten egg, Parmesan cheese, and Italian seasoning.
  4. Layer the lasagna:
    • Spread a thin layer of meat sauce on the bottom of a 9x13-inch baking dish.
    • Add a layer of lasagna noodles.
    • Spread a portion of the ricotta cheese mixture over the noodles.
    • Sprinkle with shredded mozzarella.
    • Spoon over more meat sauce.
    • Repeat the layers until you run out of ingredients, finishing with a final layer of noodles, sauce, and a heavy blanket of mozzarella and Parmesan.
  5. Bake: Cover the dish with aluminum foil (use toothpicks so the foil does not stick to the cheese). Bake at 375°F for 45 minutes. Remove the foil, increase the heat to 450°F (or broil for 3–5 minutes), and bake for another 10 to 15 minutes until bubbly and golden.
  6. Rest and serve: Let the lasagna rest for 15 to 20 minutes before slicing so the layers set properly.
